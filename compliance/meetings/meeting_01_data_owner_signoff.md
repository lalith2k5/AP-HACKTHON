# Meeting - Data Owner Sign-Off
Duration: 30 minutes
Attendees: You, Data Owner
Goal: Signed 05_data_owner_signoff.md

## Agenda

0:00-0:03  Context (3 min)
  - System: StoreSignal AI, internal retail ops intelligence
  - Why now: pilot approved, need sign-off before loading real data
  - What we are asking: sign the one-page form

0:03-0:15  Walk the form (12 min)
  - Data classes table: 6 rows, retention window per row
  - Access matrix: 7 roles, read/write per role
  - 6 declarations - read each aloud, ask for confirmation

0:15-0:25  Questions (10 min)
  - Anticipate: lawful basis, DPDP erasure vs append-only audit,
    SAR procedure, on-premise storage requirement
  - Offer: follow-up with legal counsel if any answer is unclear
  - Do NOT promise legal advice you cannot give

0:25-0:30  Sign and next steps (5 min)
  - Ask for signature on the spot
  - If they want to review first: agree a deadline (48h), leave the form
  - Note any conditions in the sign-off block

## What to bring
- Printed 05_data_owner_signoff.md (2 copies)
- Printed DATA_INVENTORY.md as backup detail
- Laptop with the running app for a 2-minute live walkthrough if asked

## Do NOT
- Do not say "we are compliant". Say "we have documented our processing
  and are asking for your sign-off on the details."
- Do not guess at legal answers. "I will follow up with counsel" is fine.
- Do not leave without a firm next step.
