# Meeting - Identity + Legal Kickoff (combined, optional)
Duration: 30 minutes
Attendees: You, Identity team lead, Counsel
Goal: Agree scope and timelines for the two open workstreams

## Agenda

0:00-0:10  Identity (10 min)
  - Walk 04_idp_swap_guide.md
  - Ask: which provider? (Auth0, Google, Azure, Okta)
  - Ask: what role claim will carry our roles?
  - Ask: what is the expected registration turnaround?
  - Agree: who provides what, by when

0:10-0:25  Legal (15 min)
  - Walk 01_legal_review_brief.md + 02_regulatory_checklist.md
  - Ask: is DPDP, FSSAI, POSH, Consumer Protection the right scope?
  - Ask: what additional input does counsel need to give an opinion?
  - Ask: what is the proposed timeline and engagement form?
  - Agree: counsel to send written questions if anything is ambiguous

0:25-0:30  Wrap (5 min)
  - Two deliverables: signed legal opinion + working SSO
  - Two deadlines (agree now)
  - One owner per workstream

## What to bring
- 01_legal_review_brief.md
- 02_regulatory_checklist.md
- 04_idp_swap_guide.md
- DATA_INVENTORY.md, RETENTION_POLICY.md, PRIVACY_NOTES.md as backup

## Do NOT
- Do not ask counsel for a verbal opinion. Written only.
- Do not pressure on timeline. Quality > speed for legal.
- Do not let SSO registration slip past one week.
